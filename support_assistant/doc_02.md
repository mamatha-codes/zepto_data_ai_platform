# Return and Refund Policy

## Return Requests
Customers may report eligible issues with products through the available support channel.

## Damaged Products
If a product is damaged, the customer should report the issue as soon as possible and provide the requested details or evidence.

## Incorrect Products
If a customer receives an incorrect product, the issue should be reported through support for resolution.

## Refund Processing
When a refund is approved, the refund is processed according to the applicable payment method and refund process.

## Refund Timing
The time required for a refund to appear in the customer's account can vary depending on the payment method and financial institution.

## Non-Eligible Requests
Refund or return eligibility may depend on the product, order status, applicable policy, and circumstances of the request.

## General Rule
Customers should contact support with their order details when they need help with a return or refund.
