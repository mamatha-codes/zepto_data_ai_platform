# Delivery Policy

## Delivery Time
Zepto aims to deliver orders within the estimated delivery time shown in the app at checkout.

## Delivery Delays
Delivery times may change because of traffic, weather, high order volume, or other operational conditions.

## Delivery Address
Customers should provide an accurate delivery address and ensure that the delivery partner can access the location.

## Missing or Incorrect Items
If an item is missing or incorrect, the customer should report the issue through the Zepto support channel as soon as possible.

## Delivery Charges
Any applicable delivery or handling charges are displayed to the customer before the order is placed.

## General Rule
Delivery estimates are not guaranteed and may vary depending on local conditions and order availability.
