# Support Hours Policy

## Support Availability
Customer support availability may depend on the support channel and service currently provided through the app.

## In-App Support
Customers can use the available help or support options in the app to report issues and request assistance.

## Support Hours
If specific support hours apply to a support channel, the applicable availability is displayed to customers through the relevant support interface.

## Response Time
The time required to receive a response may vary depending on the type of request, support channel, and current support volume.

## Urgent Order Issues
Customers with an active order issue should provide their order details when contacting support so that the issue can be reviewed.

## General Rule
Customers should check the support section in the app for the latest information about available support channels and their operating hours.
