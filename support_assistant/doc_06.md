# Gift Card Policy

## Gift Card Purchase
Gift cards may be purchased through the available gift card options provided by the platform.

## Gift Card Redemption
Customers can redeem an eligible gift card according to the redemption instructions provided with the gift card.

## Gift Card Balance
The available gift card balance can be checked through the applicable gift card or account interface.

## Gift Card Validity
Gift cards may have an applicable validity period. Customers should check the validity details provided with the gift card.

## Gift Card Refunds
Gift card refunds or cancellations depend on the applicable gift card terms and the circumstances of the request.

## Gift Card Issues
If a gift card does not work as expected, the customer should contact support and provide the relevant gift card or order details.

## General Rule
Customers should follow the terms displayed for their specific gift card when purchasing or redeeming it.
