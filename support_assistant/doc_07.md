# Payment Policy

## Payment Methods
Customers can use the payment methods that are available and displayed during checkout.

## Payment Confirmation
The payment status should be checked in the app after completing a payment.

## Failed Payments
If a payment fails, the customer can retry using an available payment method.

## Duplicate Charges
If a customer believes they have been charged more than once for the same order, they should contact support with the relevant payment and order details.

## Payment Refunds
When a refund is approved, the refund is processed according to the applicable payment method and refund process.

## Payment Issues
Customers should contact support if the payment status is unclear or if an unexpected payment issue occurs.

## General Rule
Customers should rely on the payment status and transaction information displayed in the app.
