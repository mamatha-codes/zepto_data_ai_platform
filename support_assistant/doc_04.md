# Membership Policy

## Membership Availability
Membership benefits and availability depend on the membership plan currently offered to the customer.

## Membership Benefits
Eligible members may receive benefits such as applicable delivery-related benefits, offers, or other benefits described for their plan.

## Membership Charges
The applicable membership price is displayed before the customer confirms the membership purchase.

## Membership Validity
Membership benefits are available only during the active membership period.

## Renewal
If a membership plan supports renewal, the applicable renewal terms are shown to the customer before renewal.

## Cancellation
Membership cancellation and refund eligibility depend on the applicable membership terms and the customer's membership status.

## General Rule
Customers should check the membership details shown in the app for the benefits, price, validity, and applicable terms of their plan.
