# Tracking Policy

## Order Tracking
Customers can track eligible orders using the order tracking information available in the app.

## Tracking Information
The tracking status may include information about order preparation, dispatch, and delivery progress.

## Status Updates
Tracking information may take some time to update as the order moves through different stages.

## Delivery Partner
When delivery information is available, customers may be able to view relevant delivery status information through the app.

## Delayed Tracking
If tracking information has not changed for an extended period, the customer can contact support with the order details.

## General Rule
The latest tracking information displayed in the app should be used as the current order status.
